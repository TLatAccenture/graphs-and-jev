# Migration-First Graphs + Jev Homepage Design

## Decision

Reorder and rewrite the homepage around one primary promise:

> Turn data migration into an AI accelerator.

The mechanism is: MCP reaches fragmented source systems; a source-side decision layer sifts before movement; Google Cloud provides governed landing and activation; graphs connect the facts; Jev bounds routine decisions; Gemini receives the exceptions and evidence worth deeper reasoning.

This is a whole-page editorial redesign, not merely a new inserted section. Valuable demonstrations and technical evidence remain, but dense wording, duplication and weak ordering may change. The Palindrome attribution is removed with no replacement footer copy.

## Audience and progression

The page serves executives first and enterprise architects immediately beneath:

1. **Outcome:** migration becomes an AI accelerator.
2. **Data mechanism:** graphs and decision models turn fragmented data into governed action.
3. **Agent benefit:** agents spend reasoning on ambiguity, synthesis, planning and novel work.
4. **Proof:** live Graphs + Jev examples and honest boundaries.
5. **Architecture:** Google Cloud migration, modernisation, activation and acceleration.
6. **Depth:** complete technical guide, Spanner Graph spotlight, comparisons, labs, queries and sources.

## Story order

1. **Hero — Turn data migration into an AI accelerator.** Plain supporting copy names MCP, source-side sifting, Google Cloud, graphs, decision models and Gemini without defining every term.
2. **Four-stage visual spine — Migration → Modernisation → Activation → Acceleration.** Cloud-neutral sources; MCP; source-side fan; governed Cloud Storage; Sensitive Data Protection and Dataplex after landing; BigQuery and Spanner Graph; Jev; Vertex AI Gemini.
3. **One decision, shown end to end.** Preserve the guided graph example but rewrite its introduction around evidence becoming action.
4. **Three worked applications.** Preserve DCCEEW, Parkinson’s and Little Bunnies, with shorter business-question / graph / decision framing.
5. **Why decision models matter.** Eight-beat scroll story and illustrative work-unit model; no pricing or savings claims.
6. **Well-architected agent setup.** MCP → Evidence → Relationship → Decision → Reasoning, with Assurance cross-cutting and six explained business outcomes.
7. **Trust and boundaries.** Traceability, refusal, human review, explicit egress and honest scope.
8. **Technical guide.** Preserve the full Spanner Graph spotlight, engine grades/costs, labs, query dialects, local runs and sources behind progressive disclosure.

## Visual system

Keep the current Instrument Sans / JetBrains Mono identity, Accenture purple, black, white, warm paper and semantic evidence/answer/review/reject colours. Google blue marks Google Cloud placement only. Use ruled editorial layouts, square geometry and the existing evidence-path language. No gradients, glass, shadows, generic SaaS cards or ArtCraft branding.

The same work/data tiles move through the visual mechanisms. ArtCraft contributes principles only: show instead of tell, expose layers, preserve history, and provide quick exploration followed by precise controls.

## Interaction and accessibility

The work-allocation story is positional and idempotent. On normal-motion desktop, a sticky scene changes one mark per 96vh beat; previous states fade but remain. A clickable 1–8 rail, arrow keys, previous/next controls, an expanded linear view and an aria-live status expose the same state. Reduced motion defaults to a non-sticky 90svh linear sequence with zero transitions; users may explicitly preview motion and return to system preference. Mobile is full-width, content-sized and overflow-free.

The workload model uses work units only. Counts are nonnegative and sum exactly at every reachable slider position. It is labelled “Illustrative operating model—not a benchmark,” and says filtering reduces reasoning work only when the workflow avoids or reduces general-model calls.

## Content rules

- One section, one claim, one visual job.
- Executive sentence before architectural mechanism.
- Introduce a term only when needed.
- Use the fixed outcomes: Answer, Clarify, Reject, Review, No data.
- Reasoning is routed, never described as bypassing control.
- Source-side sifting complements, not replaces, Sensitive Data Protection and Dataplex after landing.
- Gemini sits inside the Google Cloud acceleration plane and is not coloured with semantic answer green.
- No unsupported quantitative claims.
- Remove the Palindrome attribution and empty footer.
