Use case · Research evidence

Trace Parkinson’s research back to its source.

A question about Parkinson’s research uses live public-source retrieval from Wikipedia and Europe PMC. The graph keeps each relationship a retrieved page states, typed and tied to its citation. Jev answers only when a page states a relationship, and refuses clinical requests before any search runs.

Research retrieval, not clinical advice.This page does not diagnose, prescribe, or recommend a dose or treatment. Questions that ask for any of these are refused. Speak to a qualified clinician about care.

Business question
What does published work state about this research question, and where does it say so?
Graph contribution
Turns retrieved pages into typed relationships, such as gene associates disease, each tied to the page that states it.
Jev control
Answers only from a stated relationship. Otherwise it asks for a narrower question, reports no data, or refuses.
Safety boundary
Dose, diagnosis and prescription requests are refused before retrieval. Nothing is fetched for them.
